Assembly order

  1. Cut the base; mount bearing block and motor A on slotted holes.
  2. Fit shaft, pulley, hub and platen; fit belt, tension it and mark the 0° line.
  3. Fix both posts, insert rods and the lead screw with the carriage on them.
  4. Mount motor B and coupler; fit home switch, ruler and servo.
  5. Wire the circuit (see circuit), run H to home, then calibrate steps per mm and per revolution.
